<b>Project: </b> PetsChef

<b>Description: </b> A blog site that lets pet owners create, read, update, and delete posts about the food they are serving their pets. 

<b>About:</b> Many pet owners love to talk about their animals, and there are several blogs dedicated just for that. Meanwhile many chefs have food blogs for talking about their favorite recipes. But what about the chefs who also happen to be pet owners? Where can they go to show off the special dishes they cooked up for their furry friends? Here comes PetsChef! A simple blog site which helps a community of pet owners show off the delicious meal recipes their pets love! Showing the best in what pet cuisine can offer. All pets are welcome!

<b>Features:</b>
View blog posts
Upload images of the food along with text as a caption in the post
Delete posts
Write comments on a post

<b>Tech Stack:</b>
Vanilla HTML, CSS, and Javascript on Frontend
Node.JS and Express.JS on the backend to handle routing and server responses 
No database, all data locally stored
